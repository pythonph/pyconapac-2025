Volunteers

Every great event is built on the passion and dedication of its people, and PyCon APAC 2025 is no exception. This year, we embrace the theme "Haligi"—the Filipino word for "pillar"—symbolizing the unwavering support and strength of our community.

Behind every insightful talk, engaging panel, and seamless experience stands a team of extraordinary volunteers. These are the individuals who bring PyCon APAC to life—organizing, planning, and ensuring that every attendee has an unforgettable experience. They give their time, energy, and expertise, not for monetary gain, but for something far more valuable: the joy of giving back, the thrill of learning, and the bonds of friendship that last a lifetime.

As we come together to celebrate Python and its vibrant community, let's take a moment to recognize and thank the people who make it all possible. Their dedication is what keeps the PyCon spirit alive and thriving.
